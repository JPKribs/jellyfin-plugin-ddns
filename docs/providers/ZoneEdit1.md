# ZoneEdit

On the **Domains** tab, set **Provider** to **ZoneEdit**, then fill in:

- **Hostname**: the host to update.
- **Username**: your ZoneEdit username.
- **DDNS token**: your ZoneEdit Dynamic DNS authentication token, or your account password.
- **Zone**: optional. The domain to scope the update to.
- **Server override**: leave blank to use ZoneEdit's own server.

_Ported from ddclient's `nic_zoneedit1_update`._
