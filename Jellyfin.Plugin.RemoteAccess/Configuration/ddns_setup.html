Setup

Work through these steps to reach Jellyfin from outside your network.
Checking...

  1. Proxy on

  2. Address

    Name
    Points to
    This server
    The names people use to reach Jellyfin, kept pointed here by your Domains records.
    Names without a Domains record, one per line.
    No name yet? Add a record on the Domains tab.

  3. Certificate

    Nothing is requested until you accept the Let's Encrypt Subscriber Agreement.
    Change the certificate on the Proxy tab.

  4. Router

    Called port forwarding or NAT on most routers.
    Asks the router to forward ports 80 and 443 to this server through UPnP or NAT-PMP. Saved as soon as you change it.

  5. Trust the Proxy

  6. Reachable from outside

    This asks check-host.net to connect to your address from other countries, so your address is sent to that service.
